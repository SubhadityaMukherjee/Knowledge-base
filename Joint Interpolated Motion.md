# Joint Interpolated Motion
- A method of coordinating the movement of the joints, such that all joints arrive at the desired location simultaneously. This method of [Servo Control](./Servo%2520Control.md#) produces a predictable path regardless of speed and results in the fastest [Pick and Place Cycle](./Pick%2520and%2520Place%2520Cycle.md#) time for a particular move.
