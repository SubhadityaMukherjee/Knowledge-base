# My Art

## About My Art

This page showcases my digital and traditional artwork. All pieces are hand-drawn (even the digital ones) - there's no AI-generated art here.

**Mediums I use:**
- **Digital**: [Procreate](https://procreate.com/) on iPad
- **Traditional**: Gouache (Windsor & Newton / Himi gouache)

**My Process:**
- Digital: Hard round brushes with color jitter and pressure opacity/flow, Photoshop chalk brush for blending, paper texture on multiply
- Traditional: Gouache painting techniques
