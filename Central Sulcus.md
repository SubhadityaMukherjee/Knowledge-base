# Central [[./Sulcus|Sulcus]]
- The primary groove in the brain’s [[./Cerebrum|Cerebrum]], which separates the [[./Frontal lobe|Frontal lobe]] in the front of the brain from the parietal and occipital lobes in the rear of the brain.
